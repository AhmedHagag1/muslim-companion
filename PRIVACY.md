# Privacy

Developer: Ahmed Haggag. Contact: ahmedhaggagdev@gmail.com.

Muslim Companion has no account, ads, analytics, cloud sync, data sale, or AI provider. Quran activity, preferences, bookmarks, memorization, Khatma, settings, backup and Ask processing are local. Backups move only when the user explicitly exports/imports them.

Location is permission-gated for prayer/Qibla. Prayer refresh may send coordinates, date and calculation settings to AlAdhan. Recitation requests EveryAyah URLs. These services receive normal network metadata such as IP address. Notifications are scheduled locally and audio may continue in the background. Ask transmits no location or personal state.

A web-hostable policy is prepared at `docs/store/PRIVACY_POLICY_WEB.md`; no hosted URL is claimed yet.
