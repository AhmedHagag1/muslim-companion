# Muslim Companion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 August 2026. Developer: Ahmed Haggag. Contact: ahmedhaggagdev@gmail.com.

Muslim Companion does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, data sale, or AI provider. Quran activity, bookmarks, memorization, Khatma, settings and Ask processing are stored locally on the device. User-created backups are exported or imported only on the user's instruction.

With permission, precise location is used for prayer times and Qibla. Prayer refresh may send coordinates, date and calculation choices to AlAdhan; its server also receives ordinary network metadata such as IP address. Recitation streams from EveryAyah, which receives IP address and the requested reciter/ayah URL. The app schedules local notifications and may play background audio. Location and personal state are not included in backups or sent to Ask.

Uninstalling normally removes local app data subject to operating-system backup behavior. Third-party retention is governed by those services and is not controlled by the developer. To ask a privacy question or request support, email ahmedhaggagdev@gmail.com.
